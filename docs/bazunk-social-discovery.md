# Bazunk Social discovery and monetisation

Enable **Social discovery feeds** and each required source feature in the Social admin toggles. For You uses category preferences, followed accounts and authors of saved posts. Trending uses recent posts and engagement, with market filters based on the associated listings. Market filters are currently UK and US. Content without a market-tagged listing appears under All markets.

In **Content → Social discovery**, admins can:

- Add a sponsorship to an active Story or published Short using the content ID in its discovery link. Enter the sponsor name that customers should see. Book and settle sponsorships separately through the existing advertising process.
- Feature an approved creator by storefront ID.
- Set prices for 24-hour boosts. Defaults are 99 credits for a post and 199 for a Short. Members buy boosts under My content. The price shown at confirmation is checked before debiting.
- Create time-limited shopping challenges with a maximum number of claims and a credit reward. Each account can claim once per challenge using an eligible approved Short published during its window. A Short can only be used for one claim.
- Grant credits to approved creators. Awards are visible in Creator Studio and on the credit ledger.

Keep the relevant Social switches off until the Shorts dependency and creator storefronts have passed their live checks. Credit spend, claims and creator grants also require the existing Credits feature. The public creator sitemap includes approved storefronts when Social and Creator Storefronts are enabled.
